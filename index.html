A Quick Heads-Up: The analysis, mapping, and logic used here represent the developer's personal opinions, interpretations, and independent research. Because language testing algorithms are complex and constantly evolving, the insights provided here might not be 100% accurate and should be used as a general guide rather than an official diagnostic.

🧠 How the Logic Works

We want to take the guesswork out of your preparation! The tool uses a specific deduction engine to highlight where you can gain the most traction:

  • The Focus Threshold: Any subprofile skill score less than your target benchmark is flagged as a potential area for growth.
  • Smart Mapping: We cross-reference the 22 PTE question types against their related subskills. You can see exactly how these connections are made by checking out our Skills-to-Question-Type Matrix Table on this page.
  • The Deduction Engine: Instead of just giving you generic advice, the tool looks for patterns. If you have asymmetric scores (for example: perfect transcribing but a low overall reproducing score), it tries to isolate the exact task causing the bottleneck. If multiple areas are low, it will gently highlight all related categories so you don't miss a thing.

📊 How Your Tasks Are Categorised

To make your study plan easy to prioritise, we group question types into three buckets:

  • 🔴 Strong Focus Required: Question types where all mapped subskills scored below your target benchmark. These are your biggest opportunities for breakthrough!
  • 🟡 Requires Attention: Question types mapped to a mix of both high and low subskills.
  • 🟢 Strong / Good: Question types where all contributing subskills met or exceeded your target benchmark. Keep doing what you're doing here!

🔒 Privacy & Security (Your Data is Safe)

Studying for exams is stressful enough and you shouldn't have to worry about your data. This tool operates 100% locally in your web browser. None of your scorecard data, text, or pasted JSON is ever stored, uploaded, or transmitted to a server. Everything is computed instantly on your own device and vanishes the moment you close the tab.
